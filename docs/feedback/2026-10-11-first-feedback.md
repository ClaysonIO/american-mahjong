# First user feedback (2026-10-11)

## What they said

> I think for an Easy/Learning Mode that actually is perfect. I would suggest starting
> everyone with thirteen tiles instead of fourteen, with the 14th tile drawn being the
> winning tile that does not require a discard, and for the card selection, lock the tiles
> on the top and scroll up and down the card with the ability to select 2 line options at
> a time. Players can go back to card selection and pick a new line at any time, but
> outside of the easy/learning mode they aren't suggested.
>
> Keep the '8 tiles to win' on the easy mode but remove it from the regular play.

## Actionable items

| # | Item | How we read it | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Two modes: **Easy / Learning** and **Regular** | The game as it was, with all its hints, becomes Easy / Learning. Regular play takes the hints away. The mode is picked at the top of the screen and remembered. | Done |
| 2 | **Everyone starts with 13 tiles** | East no longer gets 14. Every turn, East's first one included, starts with a draw. Applies to both modes. | Done |
| 3 | **The 14th tile can be the winning tile, with no discard** | When the tile you draw (or call) completes a hand, you declare Mahjong instead of discarding. The turn prompt now says so when it happens. | Done |
| 4 | **Card selection screen** | A screen showing the whole card, which scrolls up and down while your tiles stay fixed at the top. It opens after the deal. | Done |
| 5 | **Pick 2 lines at a time** | Up to two hands ("lines") from the card can be selected as your targets. Selecting a third asks you to unselect one first. | Done |
| 6 | **Change lines at any time** | The **Card** button (and **Change lines** next to your lines) reopens the card selection at any point in the game. | Done |
| 7 | **Suggestions only in Easy / Learning** | Easy mode marks the closest lines as *Suggested* on the card selection screen and suggests a discard that fits your chosen lines. Regular mode shows neither. | Done |
| 8 | **"8 tiles to win" only in Easy / Learning** | The count of tiles still needed (per line on the card selection screen and for your chosen lines) shows only in Easy mode. Regular mode shows just the lines you picked. | Done |

## Not changed

- Regular mode still enables the **Mahjong!** button only when your hand is complete;
  the game doesn't let you declare a hand that isn't on the card (there are no penalties
  for a false Mahjong yet).
- The computer players don't use the card selection screen; they keep choosing their
  hands the way they did before.
