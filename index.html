American Mahjong

    The Card

    Colors are suits: first, second, third (any suits, all different). X Y Z Q = any consecutive numbers. D = the dragon of that suit, 0 = Soap. C = concealed, X = exposed allowed.

    How to play

    Make one of the hands on the Card with 14 tiles. The 152 tiles are three suits (Bam, Crak, Dot 1–9), winds, dragons, 8 flowers and 8 jokers. Each dragon goes with a suit: Green–Bam, Red–Crak, Soap (White)–Dot; Soap is also used as 0.

    1. Charleston. Before play everyone passes 3 tiles right, across, then left. Anyone may stop the second Charleston (left, across, right). Jokers can't be passed.
    2. Play. East discards first. On your turn you draw a tile, then discard one. Click a tile, then Discard (or click it again).
    3. Calling. When someone discards a tile you can call it to complete a group of 3 or more (pung, kong, quint) and expose it, or to win. You can't call for a pair or single unless it wins. Concealed hands (C) may only call the winning tile.
    4. Jokers stand in for any tile in a group of 3 or more, never in a pair or single. On your turn, if someone has a joker in an exposed group and you hold the real tile, select it and press Swap for joker.
    5. Mahjong! Press it when your 14 tiles match the card. The discarder pays double, a self-drawn win is paid double by everyone, and a hand with no jokers doubles again.

    This is a practice card written for this game, not the official National Mah Jongg League card. Simplified: no blind passes, courtesy pass or dead hands.
