American Mahjong

    Pick your lines

    Pick up to 2 lines (hands) from the card to aim for; you can change them at any time with the Card button. Colors are suits: first, second, third (any suits, all different). X Y Z Q = any consecutive numbers. D = the dragon of that suit, 0 = Soap. C = concealed, X = exposed allowed.

    How to play

    Make one of the hands (lines) on the Card with 14 tiles. Everyone is dealt 13 and picks up to 2 lines to aim for (change them any time with Card). The 152 tiles are three suits (Bam, Crak, Dot 1–9), winds, dragons, 8 flowers and 8 jokers. Each dragon goes with a suit: Green–Bam, Red–Crak, Soap (White)–Dot; Soap is also used as 0.

    1. Charleston. Before play everyone passes 3 tiles right, across, then left. Anyone may stop the second Charleston (left, across, right). Jokers can't be passed.
    2. Play. East goes first. On your turn you draw a tile, then discard one. Click a tile, then Discard (or click it again). Drag tiles to arrange your hand however you like. If the tile you draw completes your hand, that's Mahjong: no discard needed.
    3. Calling. When someone discards a tile you can call it to complete a group of 3 or more (pung, kong, quint) and expose it, or to win. You can't call for a pair or single unless it wins. Concealed hands (C) may only call the winning tile.
    4. Jokers stand in for any tile in a group of 3 or more, never in a pair or single. On your turn, if someone has a joker in an exposed group and you hold the real tile, select it and press Swap for joker.
    5. Mahjong! Press it when your 14 tiles match the card. The discarder pays double, a self-drawn win is paid double by everyone, and a hand with no jokers doubles again.

    Your game is saved as you play, so a refresh picks up where you left off. Finished games are kept under History to look back over.

    Easy / Learning mode suggests lines and discards and shows how many tiles you need to win. Regular mode leaves that to you.

    This is a practice card written for this game, not the official National Mah Jongg League card. Simplified: no blind passes, courtesy pass or dead hands.
